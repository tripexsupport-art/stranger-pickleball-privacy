Delete Your Account

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Stranger Pickleball account and the data associated with it, published by Tripex.

How to Request Deletion

In the app, open Profile → Request account deletion to open this process or contact support below.

Cancel Plus separately. Deleting your Stranger Pickleball account or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play to stop future renewals.
  1. Send an email to tripexsupport@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account (or your Google account email, if you signed in with Google).
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account".
  3. Include the display name shown on your profile so we can locate the correct account.
We aim to confirm your request within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days, as described in our Privacy Policy.

What Gets Deleted

DataOutcome
Account credentials (email, Google sign-in link)Deleted
Profile (display name, photo, composition/skill preference)Deleted
Sessions you hosted or joined, and slot claimsDeleted
Invitations you sent or receivedDeleted
Your attendance records and reliability ratingDeleted
Push notification tokenDeleted

What May Be Retained, and For How Long

Attendance facts that contribute to other players' reliability ratings may remain without a link to your profile. Limited purchase verification, fraud prevention, dispute and legally required transaction records may be retained separately for as long as necessary for those purposes. Google Play and advertising providers handle their own records under their policies. Your deletion confirmation will explain any applicable retention exception.

Questions

For anything about this process, contact tripexsupport@gmail.com. See also our full Privacy Policy.
