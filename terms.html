Stranger Plus subscription terms

Last updated: 20 October 2026. These terms apply where Stranger Plus is offered. Subscriptions are not yet available in the current closed-test release.

Monthly plan

Where available, Stranger Plus is an optional monthly auto-renewing Google Play subscription. The app and Google Play checkout show the localized price, currency and payment terms before purchase. There is no advertised free trial, annual plan or introductory offer in this release.

  • No displayed advertisements while Plus is verified and active.
  • 30 new hosted games per UTC calendar month and up to 10 active hosted games.
  • 20 active invitations per game and 50 player searches per UTC day, with up to 20 displayed results per search.

Free users retain 4 new hosted games per UTC calendar month, 2 active hosted games, 5 active invitations per game and 10 searches per UTC day. Joining, reliability facts, blocking, reporting and account/privacy tools remain free. Unused allowances do not roll over. Canceling a hosted game does not return its monthly hosting credit.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Google Play handles payment and automatic renewal until you cancel. Manage or cancel through the app's Google Play subscription link or Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation normally stops the next renewal while access continues through the paid period. Any refund, statutory rights, renewal notice or price-change requirements follow Google Play's terms and applicable law; these terms do not exclude those rights.

Verification and restoration

Plus is tied to the signed-in player account and a verified Google Play purchase. Restore purchases while signed into the same player and Play accounts. Pending payments do not grant Plus. Expiry, payment hold, suspension or revocation can return the account to free limits. If verification is unavailable, do not purchase a second subscription: restore again later or contact support.

A downgrade does not erase your existing game commitments. You can still leave or cancel a game, confirm attendance and use safety/account tools. Higher limits apply to new actions only while Plus remains verified.

An internet connection and periodic Google Play verification are required for Plus features. If verification cannot be refreshed within 24 hours, higher limits and the verified subscription state may be temporarily unavailable until purchases can be restored.

Account deletion

Deleting your player account or uninstalling the app does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel renewals in Google Play separately. See account deletion and the privacy policy. Contact support before deleting an account if you need help with an existing subscription.

Service and support

Plus does not guarantee available games, attendance, opponents, court bookings or uninterrupted connectivity. Reliability facts cannot be purchased or improved by subscribing. Court fees are arranged directly between hosts and players, outside the app.

The service has shared capacity limits. Features, including paid features, may temporarily stop when those limits are reached. A subscription increases your personal allowances; it does not reserve server capacity or guarantee uninterrupted service. Contact support if an interruption affects your subscription.
