Stranger Pickleball

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Stranger Pickleball is a matchmaking app that lets hosts post open pickleball sessions and lets nearby players claim or get invited to open slots. This policy describes what information the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

1 · Information We Collect

Account & profile

  • Email address and display name, via Firebase Authentication (email/password or Google Sign-In)
  • Your profile preferences and the profile-photo URL supplied by your Google account, if present. The current app does not provide custom photo uploads.

Location

  • Foreground location permission can provide a precise GPS position when you choose to set your area or a game's location. Your player profile stores a coarse area geohash. A posted game includes its venue text and coordinates, visible to signed-in players. We do not continuously track location or request background-location access.

Session & activity data

  • Sessions you host or join: venue, date/time, cost, composition, and slot claims
  • Invitations sent and received between hosts and players
  • Attendance records (hosted / completed / no-show) used to calculate your reliability rating — a factual, non-skill-based score visible to other users

Device & notifications

  • A push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can notify you of invitations and session updates
  • Crash and diagnostic reports through Firebase Crashlytics to investigate failures and improve reliability

Subscription and usage records

  • Google Play purchase tokens, the subscription product, verification state and expiry, and a pseudonymous account identifier used to bind a purchase to your player account
  • Hosting, invitation and search records used to enforce your plan's limits and prevent duplicate or abusive operations

2 · What We Do Not Collect

  • Payment card or bank details: Google Play handles optional subscription payments. We receive purchase-verification information, not your card number. Court/session costs are still settled directly between players and hosts outside the app.
  • Precise continuous background location tracking
  • Skill-based ratings or performance scores — reliability ratings are based only on attendance facts (hosted / completed / no-show)

3 · How Your Data Is Used

  • To match you with nearby sessions and players, and to operate the claim/invite workflow
  • To calculate and display reliability ratings. These counters are written only by trusted server code — never directly by any user's device — to keep them trustworthy
  • To send you push notifications about invitations, claims, and session changes

4 · Advertising & Subscriptions

In releases where advertising is enabled, free users may see a Google AdMob banner on the discovery screen. The app requests non-personalized advertisements. Google's SDK can still collect or share an IP address (which can indicate a general location), device/account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalized does not mean no data collection.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before advertisements can be requested. An Ad privacy choices action is available when required. Declining consent, an unavailable advertisement or a connection failure does not prevent core game, safety or account actions. You can also manage your advertising identifier in Android settings.

Where offered, Stranger Plus is an optional monthly Google Play subscription with higher hosting, invitation and search allowances and no displayed advertisements while the subscription is verified and active. Its price and renewal terms appear before checkout. You can restore purchases or manage/cancel the subscription in Google Play. See the subscription terms. Development builds use test ads and can have checkout disabled; availability is shown in the app.

5 · Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Cloudflare Workers — our server processes authenticated requests, player and game information, invitation notification delivery, usage limits and Google Play verification records. Request processing includes network information such as IP addresses. Purchase tokens and private player fields are not exposed to other players. Cloudflare privacy policy
  • Google Sign-In — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — Google advertising privacy
  • Google Play Billing — Google privacy policy

6 · Data Sharing Between Users

Your shared profile (display name, photo, gender, badges and reliability facts) and posted game details can be shown to signed-in players through discovery, games and invitations. Private email addresses, block lists, push tokens and purchase tokens are not part of public player profiles. Advertising data is handled by Google as described in Section 4; we do not send purchase tokens or private block lists to AdMob.

7 · Data Retention & Deletion

Your account and associated data are retained while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data using the contact details below; we aim to complete eligible deletion requests within 30 days, subject to any required retention described below.

Use Request account deletion in the app or the deletion request page. Limited purchase, fraud-prevention or legally required records may need to be retained for those purposes. We do not automatically erase records when a subscription ends. Deleting an account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel future renewals in Google Play separately. Google controls retention of records held in its own services under its policies.

8 · Data Security

App records are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). Client access is protected by security rules; trusted Cloudflare server requests use restricted server credentials and authenticate the player before carrying out an authorized action. Reliability and subscription records are protected against direct client modification. Server credentials are never included in the Android app.

9 · Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL. Material changes will be noted by updating the date at the top of the page.
